Hi, I'm Utkarsh.

Welcome to my cybersecurity blog, where I document my journey through hands-on security research, Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges, penetration testing labs, and practical cybersecurity learning.

This blog is a collection of technical write-ups, methodologies, notes, and lessons learned from working through security challenges and exploring different areas of offensive security.

## What You'll Find Here

The content on this blog focuses primarily on practical cybersecurity topics, including:

- Web application security
- Vulnerability assessment
- Reconnaissance and enumeration
- Privilege escalation
- Exploitation techniques
- Capture the Flag challenges
- TryHackMe labs
- Security tools and methodologies
- Practical security research

My goal is not simply to document whether a challenge was solved, but to understand the methodology behind each step — from initial reconnaissance and identifying attack surfaces to vulnerability analysis, exploitation, and post-exploitation.

## Learning Through Practice

Cybersecurity is a constantly evolving field, and hands-on practice is one of the most effective ways to build and strengthen technical skills.

Through these write-ups, I aim to document what I learn, revisit concepts when needed, and build a reference that can be useful to others who are also developing their cybersecurity skills.

Every challenge presents an opportunity to understand something new, improve problem-solving skills, and approach security from an attacker's perspective.

## Responsible Learning

The purpose of this blog is education and knowledge sharing.

Flags and other challenge-specific answers are intentionally redacted from the write-ups. The focus is on documenting the methodology, techniques, tools, and reasoning used to reach a solution rather than simply providing the answer.

All activities documented here are performed in authorized labs, CTF environments, or other settings where security testing is permitted.
